## Synthesis (analyst note, derived only from items above)
- Pre-add signal exists only as general CEFR level and/or global frequency band (items 4, 5, 7). No tool found gives a domain-conditioned usefulness level (high/medium/limited/low) with confidence for a single word.
- Domain-specific tools (items 9, 10, 11) are fixed curated decks; domain relevance is implicit in deck membership, not a visible per-word assessment.
- Domain-statistical machinery exists (Sketch Engine keywords, Lextutor AWL/MSVL, item 1-3) but is text/corpus-level, for teachers/researchers, with no SRS link.
- Closest "worth learning for subject" claim: item 12 (marketing language, bulk extraction, school subjects).
- No product or startup found explicitly claiming to tell a learner a word is "worth learning" for their professional field (negative result limited by ~10 searches).
