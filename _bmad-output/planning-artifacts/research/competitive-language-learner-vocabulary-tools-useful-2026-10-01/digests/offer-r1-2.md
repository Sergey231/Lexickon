## Cross-tool synthesis (derived)
- Pre-add signals that exist: LingQ per-lesson % new words and blue/yellow/white status; Migaku known/unknown statuses and per-page comprehension; Language Reactor per-word frequency rank and level-based video filter. All are general-language frequency/known-ness, per-text or per-word.
- No evidence found of any tool giving a per-domain/profession usefulness assessment per word before saving. Closest: Lingvist for Business custom industry courses (B2B, sales-led).
- Platforms: Readlang, Lingvist, LingQ, Migaku have native iOS apps; Language Reactor is a desktop Chrome extension (iOS native not found).
