# Digest offer-r1-1: Anki, Clozemaster, Memrise, Vocabulary.com, Duolingo (vocab-selection signals, domain vocab, pricing, positioning)

Decision served: is a per-domain pre-save "usefulness" assessment differentiated? Angle: English learners, Russian speakers.
Caveat: source retrieval was weak. Several primary pages 404'd or returned nothing (memrise.com/pro, vocabulary.com/premium, duolingo.com/super, ankiweb shared page). Pricing is mostly from aggregators and an app-store search snippet. Budget exceeded by ~2 calls.
