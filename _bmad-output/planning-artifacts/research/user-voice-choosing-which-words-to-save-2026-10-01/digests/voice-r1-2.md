1. claim: A commenter on a Habr Anki article said manual, tedious card making is "50%" of quality memorization and warned against shared decks and others' material. 2. claim: A later commenter (Feb 2024) said manual card creation on mobile (lookup, copy between apps, formatting) is about 10 minutes per word, which is prohibitive. This is friction in saving, not in choosing. 1 voice.
   source: https://habr.com/en/articles/454236/comments | Habr | 2024-02-05 | confidence: med | class: (b) workflow friction

   source: https://habr.com/en/articles/759636 | Habr | 2023-09-08 | confidence: med | class: (b) junk-card workaround, wish for pre-made lists
   quote: "Я хочу просто взять список на 500 или 5000 слов загрузить в программу"

4. claim: Asker on Habr Q&A (3+ yrs old) rejected Duolingo for teaching unneeded words, Lingualeo for lacking repetition, Anki for needing self-population. He wants a personal dictionary, SRS, and minimal-action word adding from an e-reader. One answerer: unneeded words fall out of repetition in Anki over time. 2 voices.
   source: https://qna.habr.com/q/520024 | Habr Q&A | pub_date: unknown (older, approx 2019-2020) | confidence: med | class: (a)(b)(f)
   phrase used: "ненужные слова" (Duolingo teaches unneeded words)

5. claim: Programmers' thread on Habr Q&A: three competing methods (look up and continue, guess from context, write each unknown word into Anki). One answerer said it took about 1.5 years of daily work to read programming literature freely, and that domain vocabulary is learned much faster than general English because technical terms repeat. 2-3 voices.
   source: https://qna.habr.com/q/524509 | Habr Q&A | pub_date: unknown (approx 2019) | confidence: med | class: (a)(c)

6. claim: Linux.org.ru thread (Feb 2015): one voice recommends monolingual frequency dictionaries with context; one warns generic frequency decks lack single Russian equivalents; one says flashcards are ineffective; one says immersion plus Anki.
